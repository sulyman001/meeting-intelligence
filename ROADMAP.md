# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — AI Meeting Minutes
Foundation → Meeting Intake → Transcript Pipeline → AI Analysis → Minutes & Quality → Review → Export → Release Readiness.

## Phase 2 — Meeting Intelligence
Ask the Meeting, transcript viewer, source references, search, speaker intelligence, templates, multilingual support.

## Phase 3 — Meeting Execution
Action tracking, reminders, email/calendar/Jira/Slack/Teams integrations.

## Phase 4 — Agentic Meeting Operations
Meeting preparation, follow-up, unfinished-action tracking, recurring issue detection, recommendations, governed autonomous actions.
